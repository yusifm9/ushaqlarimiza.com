Our Story

A Student-Led Movement for Every Child

Founded in July 2023 in Baku, "Ushaqlarimiza Oyredek" is a student-led Non-Profit Youth Community (NPYC) committed to helping disadvantaged, disabled, and underprivileged children feel seen, supported, and included.

Through workshops, exhibitions, charity campaigns, and partner-supported events, our volunteers create safe spaces where children can learn new skills, meet mentors, share their creativity, and take part in community life.

Our team has grown to over 100 members across several divisions, and our work now reaches children and youth through local projects in Azerbaijan and international community initiatives.

FAQ

Common Questions

Short answers for volunteers, parents, and sponsors who want to understand how UO works.

For Volunteers

What is the age requirement to join?

We do not have a strict age limit. Each applicant is evaluated based on motivation, readiness, and performance. In general, volunteers usually start from around age 14.

How many hours per week are expected?

It depends on the role and deadlines. We stay flexible because education comes first for teenagers. On average, about 2 hours per week is enough for many volunteer roles.

Do I get an official volunteer certificate?

Yes. Active volunteers may receive an official UO certificate, and certificates can be checked on our verification page.

For Parents

Is my child supervised during events and trips?

Yes. We supervise children during events and try to include all relevant restrictions, needs, and safety notes in planning. Parents should share important information in advance so the team can prepare properly.

How are safety and photo permissions handled?

Safety and photo rules are discussed before or after events. We usually avoid posting individual children unless they ask or consent. If a child or parent does not want photos posted, they can contact us and we will handle the issue accordingly.

What educational or leadership skills will my child gain?

Children can build confidence, communication, teamwork, creativity, empathy, public speaking, and problem-solving skills through workshops, forums, and community activities.

For Sponsors & Corporate Partners

How are donated funds allocated?

Funds are directed toward event materials, learning resources, transport, food or support packages, venue needs, and other direct project costs. We prioritize practical support that reaches children.

What CSR and brand exposure benefits do partners receive?

Partners can receive event visibility, social media mentions, recap materials, partner-page recognition, and a clear community impact story. UO has experience working with partners such as YeloBank, Azercosmos, Azercell, and others.

Can our company co-host an educational workshop with UO?

Yes. We can co-design workshops with companies, schools, public institutions, and community partners around education, technology, creativity, ecology, leadership, and child support.
